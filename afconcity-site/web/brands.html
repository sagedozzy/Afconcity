Questions

What happens after I pay?

We check your design within one working day. Once approved it goes live on your start date for every player, and you get an email from us.

How do I pay?

Payment is handled by Bachs, which takes cards and mobile money in many African countries. We never see your card or wallet details.

Can I use my own artwork or logo?

Yes. After you book, email your artwork to support@afconcity.com with your booking reference and we will swap it in.

Refunds

If we decline your design, you get a full refund. See the terms for the rest.
