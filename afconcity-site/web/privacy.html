Privacy

AfconCity is a free game you play in your browser. You never give us your name, phone number or email to play. This page explains the little we do collect, why, how long we keep it and how to have it removed.

The short version

  • When you start playing, our database gives your browser a random player ID. It is not linked to your name, number or email.
  • Your game progress is saved on your own device. If you use a save code, a copy is also kept on our server.
  • The welcome questions are optional. Your answers are anonymous and help us plan events and features.
  • We count how the game is played (sessions, steps reached, device type) to make it better. There are no advertising trackers and no cookies for tracking.
  • Chat messages are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • We do not sell your data.

Young players

AfconCity is suitable for all ages. If you tell us you are under 18, the Nyota shop is hidden and top-ups are refused, and chat is limited to friendly preset phrases, so you cannot type messages to strangers or receive personal messages. We do not ask for contact details from anyone, except the name and email an adult gives for a Nyota receipt. If you are a parent or guardian and want your child's data removed, write to us (see below).

Who helps us run the game

  • Supabase hosts our database and anonymous sign-in.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website.
  • Bachs processes brand payments and Nyota top-ups.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries. Your browser asks them for files, as with any website.

These companies may process data outside your country. They work under their own privacy and security commitments.

What is kept on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to keep your progress, settings, welcome answers and the offline copy of the game. You can delete it any time by clearing site data for afconcity.com in your browser. That also removes your progress unless you have a save code.

Your rights

Wherever you live, including under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, Nigeria's Data Protection Act, Kenya's Data Protection Act and the EU's GDPR, you can ask us to show you, correct or delete the data linked to your player ID or save code. Write to support@afconcity.com with your save code or fan name and roughly when you played. We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we update this page and the date at the top. Big changes are also announced in the game.
